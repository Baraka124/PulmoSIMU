How it works & research limitations

VR controls and procedure

In anatomy mode, hold trigger or grip to move the lung. Press left Y for the spatial menu. Choose BRONCHOSCOPY to see the guided airway window and endoscopic display. At actual junctions, choose a source branch. FOCUS VIEW reduces scene clutter; EXPERT TOOLS restores instruments and guide controls.

Mixed Reality · Quest 2 passthrough preview

Open the MR workstation and then choose ENTER MR · PASSTHROUGH PREVIEW. The option appears only if Quest Browser supports immersive-ar. Grayscale passthrough is not verified tabletop anchoring, real-world occlusion or registered patient anatomy.
