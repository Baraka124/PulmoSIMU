XR-14 · Immersive Procedure Laboratory

One original pulmonary engine with a dedicated bronchoscopy guide: a live, source-labelled airway segment, segment progress, branch decisions, instrument mode, and a separate endoscope monitor. Studio and Focus views remain available. Reference-only and not clinically validated.

Procedure: Enter immersive VR, choose BRONCHOSCOPY. The source-derived guide opens below the endoscopic monitor. Advance or retract with the left stick. At actual airway junctions, point at a branch in the guide and press trigger. PREVIOUS and NEXT STEP organise a demonstration; they do not imply validated clinical progress. The guide's TOOL MODE selects original research instruments. Hide or restore the guide from Expert Tools.

Controls: Press left Y to show/hide the main spatial panel. Aim to highlight a button; trigger selects the nearest real panel surface.
