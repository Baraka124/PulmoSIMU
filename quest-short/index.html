XR-11 · Spatial anatomy and instrument laboratory

Original anatomical and bronchoscopy engine, with bounded world-size, a controller laser that stops at the nearest actual surface, interactive spatial menu, and enhanced anatomy control. Physical Quest 2 verification is still required.

Control the lung: Aim at a real surface; hold trigger or grip and physically move the controller. Right thumbstick: rotate horizontally / change depth. Left thumbstick up or down: shrink / enlarge within a comfortable limit. Both grips: two-handed rotation and resize.

Use the spatial menu: Aim at a panel button until it highlights amber, then press the trigger. The laser must stop on the menu, not pass through it. Choose RECENTER if the anatomy gets lost.
