VR controls and procedure

In anatomy mode, hold trigger or grip to move the lung. With supported hand tracking, articulated glove surfaces and an estimated VR body are visible. In Expert tools → Virtual workspace → Workspace options, toggle BODY or HAND PROCEDURE. For experimental bare-hand reference bronchoscopy, put both controllers down, open your hands, pinch near the parked handle with the right hand and pinch near the shaft below it with the left; release to stop and regrasp. The handle is visually connected to the reference airway entry; internal shaft remains source-derived. In VR Bronchoscopy → Procedure station → Patient station, enter the supine reference manikin and switch external, transparent or internal view. Approach the head-end operator zone to enable reference motion. Exit the station to restore anatomy exploration. Spatial calibration is in MR placement or Expert tools → Virtual workspace → Workspace options. Use a fixed passive pointer attached to the right controller and a measured 200 × 150 mm rectangular bench jig. Capture 5 pivot orientations, then 4 ordered landmarks. Arm each capture and press the right trigger at the physical target. No patient registration. In Imaging lab → EBUS instrument, select radial or convex reference imaging. Radial probe feed uses the left pinch/grip near the channel port or explicit feed controls; retract before changing mode. Press left Y for the spatial menu. Choose BRONCHOSCOPY to see the guided airway window and original-source endoscopic display. Use the left thumbstick or the guide ADVANCE / RETRACT controls. At actual junctions, choose a source branch. For bare hands, enable Quest hand tracking, set controllers down, then pinch at the lung or aim and pinch a control. FOCUS VIEW reduces scene clutter; EXPERT TOOLS restores instruments and guide controls.

XR-17C: On the desktop page, LOAD INSTRUMENT TRACE before entering VR to inspect an external measured bench record. In the headset choose BRONCHOSCOPY → PROCEDURE STATION → INSTRUMENT TRACE for playback, measurement residuals and the optional TRACKED TOOL PROXY (controller-only, uncalibrated). Measured simulator input requires calibration and deliberate arming; native compositor layers remain disabled in the compatibility path.

Mixed Reality · Quest 2 passthrough preview

Open the MR workstation, then choose ENTER MR · PASSTHROUGH PREVIEW. When supported, look at a real table or floor until a teal target appears and use trigger or pinch to place the reference lung. If no hit is available, choose FLOATING PREVIEW for unregistered manual placement. LEFT Y → MR PLACEMENT provides REPOSITION and an explicitly UNREGISTERED FLOATING PREVIEW if surface detection is unavailable. Quest hardware anchoring and drift are not yet tested. Grayscale passthrough is not verified tabletop anchoring, real-world occlusion or registered patient anatomy.
