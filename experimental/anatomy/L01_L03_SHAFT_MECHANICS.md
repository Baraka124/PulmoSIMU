# L01–L03 | Flexible instrument mechanics foundation

One existing Source Anatomy inspector, original reference bronchoscopy/XR unchanged. Use authorized local images and I/J sources only. No patient data in Git.

L01: uniform arc-length resampling of J route into 3–80 shaft nodes, fixed endpoints, rest length from inspiration route. Discrete stretch spring energy (N/mm) and small-deflection second-difference bending approximation (EI in N mm²), weak anchoring to candidate centerline, bounded gradient descent with backtracking. This is not an inextensible Cosserat rod, nonlinear large-deflection beam validation, torsional model, device-specific stiffness, or dynamic time integration. Numerical energy is in N mm, with assumed inputs. Maximum curvature is turning angle divided by adjacent mean length. Solver can stop at local minimum, iteration limit or failed descent; no equilibrium guarantee.

L02: on orthogonal source grids, signed distance to nearest opposite-occupancy voxel box (or outside volume) capped at radius+1 mm. Contact penalty acts at nodes, with midpoint overlap checked separately. Positive gap is a capped estimate. Gradient at voxel boundaries can be ambiguous; report unresolved nodes. Penalty stiffness is an assumption, not tissue elastic modulus. Coulomb coefficient times sum of nodal penalty magnitudes is a diagnostic only; no friction force in optimization, no measured axial insertion force, swept collision, tissue displacement, damage, or haptic command. Computation bounded to 80 nodes, 50 iterations, 8 backtracks and 8,000 candidate voxels per point; fine grids may be rejected. Browser UI uses 20 iterations.

L03: compare inspiration and expiration endpoint solutions. For expiration, map the inspiration discretization through the existing K field, retain inspiration rest segment length, and independently bind the expiration binary airway mask against exact moving CT hash and affine via I. Contact is recomputed in expiration geometry; not inferred from Jacobian. Intermediate respiratory phases are not supported for mechanics, and the coupling is one-way prescribed geometry only. No force feedback to respiration, deforming lumen, patient camera or actual physical instrument. Original candidate route remains unchanged.

Workflow: create J route, select inspiration, configure assumed stiffness and run. For expiration load K pair, then expiration mask manifest and binary mask (generated by I with expiration CT), select expiration and run. Changing source/route/field or parameters invalidates result. Export evidence JSON locally, retain separately. Plot is an orthographic shaft projection, not endoscopy or CT overlay.

Tests: node --test experimental/tests/l03-shaft-mechanics.test.mjs. Bench stiffness calibration, grid refinement, convergence, contact pressure comparisons, friction experiments and independent respiratory registration remain necessary. No clinical acceptance.
