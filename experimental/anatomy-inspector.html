1. Select the CT-derived binary GLB to inspect its meshes. 2. Select the original uncompressed NIfTI .nii CT to inspect its three source planes. 3. Select its reconstruction manifest JSON to verify dimensions, sform transform and intensity scaling.

The study remains on your device. Manual A2.3 review marks are exported only when you select Export JSON and can be re-imported only for the exact same verified CT and GLB. The gold 3D crosshair and section clipping become available only after source headers agree with the manifest. Those checks do not prove that the lung mask is clinically accurate. Browsers need internet access for the Three.js graphics libraries used by this research workstation.

For the bundled reference study, use the included GLB and manifest, then choose your original NLST NIfTI file. For a future study, its own separately documented coordinate transform is required.
