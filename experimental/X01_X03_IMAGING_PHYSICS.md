# X01–X03 | Source-linked imaging physics

Separate research instruments in the source laboratory Endoscopic optics task. The M03 renderer, candidate-search pipeline, immersive optics and reference EBUS remain unchanged.

X01: exact binary mask DDA wall intersections, relative coaxial diffuse and roughness-dependent specular response, softened inverse-square distance falloff (5 mm scale), assumed ambient, cos^4 vignette, exponential tone mapping and gamma 2.2. User adjusts relative power, diffuse reflectance and roughness; existing FOV, roll and exposure reused. No absolute radiometry, measured optics, reconstructed mucosal texture, multiple light bounces or camera calibration.

X02: straight-ray voxel traversal of all encountered mask interfaces within range, max 12 transitions and 2048 steps per ray. Lumen assumed air or ideal fluid; outside lumen homogeneous assumed medium with adjustable impedance, sound speed and attenuation. Normal-incidence pressure reflection from impedance contrast, energy transmission at prior interfaces both ways, round-trip attenuation (dB/cm/MHz), time of flight and relative amplitude. Air parameters approximate; no refraction, angular reflection, diffraction, heterogeneous tissue segmentation, multipath, wave solver, or calibrated RF. No invented tissue structures or speckle. FOV exit does not create an echo.

X03: 120 radial rays in the transverse plane or 81 lateral point-sector rays spanning 60 degrees; assumed two-cycle pulse envelope and nearest angular scan conversion, log compression with gain ramp. Display depth uses conventional 1540 m/s rather than actual ray distance, explicitly reported. Pulse width is a display approximation, not measured transducer resolution. Convex is a lateral point-source sector, not curved phased array. Bright echoes are modelled interfaces, not clinical findings. Probe origin is J route frame; no physical probe registration, extension, tissue beyond binary mask interpretation or instrument control. Export exact route SHA and per-ray echo evidence, no image upload.
