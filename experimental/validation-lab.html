Physical bench trace schema

Required fields in each sample: tMs, cmdInsertionMm, measInsertionMm, cmdTipHDeg, measTipHDeg, cmdTipVDeg, measTipVDeg, cmdRollDeg, measRollDeg, tracking. A real independent sensor and its provenance are mandatory.

Do not enter patient names, study IDs or protected clinical data. Export stays on your computer. A complete evidence packet is not a medical-device or patient-specific acceptance certificate.
